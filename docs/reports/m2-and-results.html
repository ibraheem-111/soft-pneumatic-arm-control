1 · Plant

\[ u \;\xrightarrow{\ \text{wait } T = 0.5\,\text{s}\ }\; \xrightarrow{\ \text{lag } \tau = 0.6\,\text{s}\ }\; p \;\xrightarrow{\ \text{arm, } a \approx 4.7\,\text{s}^{-1}\ }\; y \]
  • S2 → \(+y_2\), S3 → \(-y_1\), S4 → \(-y_2\). S1 is sealed: \(+y_1\) only by lowering S3.
  • 15 joint DOF, 3 inputs: underactuated. \(y\) is output-controllable: \(CAB = \Lambda H G/\tau\) has rank 3; the uncommanded internal bending is stable.

2 · Model derivation

\[ \underbrace{u \xrightarrow{\ \text{actuator}\ } p}_{\text{step 7}} \;\xrightarrow{\ \text{mechanics}\ }\; y \qquad \text{mechanics: steps 1–6,}\quad \text{both in series: step 8 (M2)} \]

1 · Coordinates

\[ q = (\theta_x,\ \theta_y,\ L)^{\mathsf T},\qquad q_0 = (0,\ 0,\ L_h)^{\mathsf T} \]

One constant-curvature arc (all levels share each segment's pressure). \(\theta_x, \theta_y\): bend angles (rad). \(L\): arc length; \(L_h\): at rest.

2 · Paper's model

\[ D_q\,\dot q + K_q\,(q - q_0) = F \]

Built on paper eq. 2 (\(\dot q = A(\rho)q + B(\rho)u + d\), planar, per section; inertia, Coriolis and gravity all lumped into \(d\)). Our version: inertia dropped as in the paper; gravity kept as stiffness in \(K_q\) (our choice); offset \(q_0\); two bending planes. \(D_q, K_q\): diagonal damping and stiffness. \(F\): bending moments and axial force.

3 · Pressure → force (what \(F\) in step 2 is)

Same model as step 2: this writes its force \(F\) in terms of the measured pressure \(p\). Column \(s\) sits at angle \(\varphi_s\) around the arm, at distance \(r\) from the centre line; \(e_s = (\cos\varphi_s,\ \sin\varphi_s)\) is the unit vector from the centre to it.

Column\(\varphi_s\)\(e_s\)Tip moves (measured)Note
S1180°\((-1,\ 0)\)\(+y_1\)Sealed; constant
S2270°\((0,\ -1)\)\(+y_2\)
S30°\((1,\ 0)\)\(-y_1\)
S490°\((0,\ 1)\)\(-y_2\)

\(e_s\) is placed opposite the measured tip direction: an inflated column lengthens, so the arm bends away from it. Assumption, not checked against the geometry; docs/simulator.md lists S1 at 0°. That only flips the sign of \(r\alpha\) in \(B_q\), and the identified \(H\) fixes the sign from data.

\[ \text{(a)}\quad \ell_s = L - r\,(e_{s,x}\theta_x + e_{s,y}\theta_y) \]

Length of column \(s\) on the arc. A fibre at distance \(r\) from the centre line is shorter on the inside of the bend and longer on the outside. Standard constant-curvature actuator-length relation (Webster & Jones, IJRR 2010).

\[ \text{(b)}\quad f_s = \alpha\,p_s \]

Force of column \(s\) along the arm. \(\alpha\): effective pouch area (N/psi). One \(\alpha\) is a simplification: the simulator uses separate bending and stretch gains; \(H\) absorbs the difference.

\[ \text{(c)}\quad F_k = \sum_s f_s\,\frac{\partial \ell_s}{\partial q_k},\qquad \frac{\partial \ell_s}{\partial \theta_x} = -r\,e_{s,x},\quad \frac{\partial \ell_s}{\partial \theta_y} = -r\,e_{s,y},\quad \frac{\partial \ell_s}{\partial L} = 1 \]

Generalized force on coordinate \(q_k\): work done by the column forces per unit change of \(q_k\) (virtual work). In the soft-robot literature this is the actuation matrix \(A(q) = (\partial\ell/\partial q)^{\mathsf T}\) (Della Santina, Duriez & Rus, IEEE CSM 2023, eq. 2).

\[ \text{(d)}\quad F = B_q\,p,\qquad B_q = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -r\alpha & 0\\ r\alpha & 0 & -r\alpha\\ \alpha & \alpha & \alpha \end{pmatrix} \]

Insert the table into (c). Columns of \(B_q\): \(p_2, p_3, p_4\). S1 is constant, so it drops out of the deviation \(p\). Row 1: only S3 bends \(\theta_x\). Row 2: S2 against S4. Row 3: all stretch \(L\).

4 · Joint level → task space (kinematics)

\[ \xi_1 = L\,\theta_x\,\tfrac{1-\cos\theta}{\theta^2},\quad \xi_2 = L\,\theta_y\,\tfrac{1-\cos\theta}{\theta^2},\quad \xi_3 = -L\,\tfrac{\sin\theta}{\theta},\qquad \theta = \sqrt{\theta_x^2 + \theta_y^2} \]

\(\xi = (\xi_1, \xi_2, \xi_3)\): tip position of an arc with bends \(\theta_x, \theta_y\) and length \(L\), hanging from the mount (constant-curvature forward kinematics).

\[ \tfrac{1-\cos\theta}{\theta^2} = \tfrac12 - \tfrac{\theta^2}{24} + \dots,\qquad \tfrac{\sin\theta}{\theta} = 1 - \tfrac{\theta^2}{6} + \dots \]

Taylor series of \(\cos\theta\) and \(\sin\theta\). At \(\theta \approx 0.05\) rad (7 mm circle on a ≈0.3 m arm) the dropped terms are 0.02 % and 0.04 %.

\[ y = \xi(q) - \xi(q_0) \approx J_0\,(q - q_0),\qquad J_0 = \frac{\partial \xi}{\partial q}\Big|_{q_0} = \operatorname{diag}\!\big(\tfrac{L_h}{2},\ \tfrac{L_h}{2},\ -1\big) \]

First-order Taylor expansion (linearization) about \(q_0\), taken as the straight hang; the real settled pose is slightly bent (\(\xi_{0,1} \approx -4.96\) mm, S1 at lower pressure), which does not affect the identified \(a, H\): they were fitted around the settled pose. The true Jacobian \(J(q)\) varies with \(q\); \(J_0\) is its value at \(q_0\), valid near it. Away from \(q_0\) off-diagonal terms grow linearly, e.g. \(\partial \xi_3/\partial\theta_x \approx L\theta_x/3\) (≈ 3 % of the diagonal at 0.05 rad): bending lifts the tip, the \(\kappa(y_1^2 + y_2^2)\) term in \(y_3\).

5 · Substitute step 4 into step 2

\[ \text{(a)}\quad D_q\,\dot q + K_q\,(q - q_0) = B_q\,p \]

Step 2 with \(F\) from step 3.

\[ \text{(b)}\quad q - q_0 = J^{-1}y,\qquad \dot q = J^{-1}\dot y \]

Step 4 inverted (\(J\) constant).

\[ \text{(c)}\quad D_q\,\dot y + K_q\,y = J B_q\,p \]

(b) into (a), multiplied by \(J\). \(J D_q J^{-1} = D_q\) and \(J K_q J^{-1} = K_q\) because diagonal matrices commute.

\[ \text{(d)}\quad \dot y = -\Lambda\,y + \Lambda H\,p,\qquad \Lambda = D_q^{-1}K_q,\qquad H = K_q^{-1}J B_q \]

(c) divided by \(D_q\). Row \(i\): \(\dot y_i = -a_i y_i + a_i (Hp)_i\), \(a_i = (K_q)_{ii}/(D_q)_{ii}\) (1/s). Steady state (\(\dot y = 0\)): \(y = Hp\), so \(H\) is mm per psi.

6 · Identified

\[ a = (4.77,\ 4.70,\ 15.5)\ \text{s}^{-1},\qquad H = \begin{pmatrix} -0.003 & -1.812 & -0.002\\ \phantom{-}1.823 & -0.002 & -1.813\\ -0.493 & -0.446 & -0.490\end{pmatrix}\ \frac{\text{mm}}{\text{psi}} \]

Fitted directly (free-run, measured \(p\) in), so \(\alpha, r, L_h, K_q, D_q\) are never needed. Zeros match \(B_q\).

7 · Actuator

\[ \tau\,\dot p_j = -p_j + g_j\,u_j(t - T)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \dot p_j = \tfrac{1}{\tau}\big(g_j u_j - p_j\big),\qquad T = 0.50\ \text{s},\ \ \tau = 0.60\ \text{s},\ \ g = (1.12,\ 1.10,\ 1.16) \]

Second block, separate from steps 1–6: command \(u\) → pressure \(p\) (the paper assumes its inner pressure loop is fast; its input is a torque/force). \(T\): dead time. \(\tau\): lag; pressure rises at a rate proportional to the remaining gap, so a step gives \(1 - e^{-(t-T)/\tau}\) (63 % at \(\tau\)). Measured: exactly this, one lag. Same form as \(\dot p = -\alpha p + \beta p_{\text{cmd}}\) (\(\alpha = 1/\tau,\ \beta = g/\tau\)) in Johnson et al., PneuDrive, arXiv:2504.00222 (2025), §II-B1, taken from Best et al., IEEE RAM 23(3) 2016. \(g_j\): regulator gain, measured psi per commanded psi (staircase: \(1.12\,u + \text{bias}\)); without it the pressure is off by 9.6–16.4 %. \(G = \operatorname{diag}(g)\).

8 · State space (M2)

\[ \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}\dot y\\ \dot p\end{pmatrix}}_{\dot X} = \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} -\Lambda & \Lambda H\\ 0 & -\tfrac{1}{\tau}I\end{pmatrix}}_{A}\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} y\\ p\end{pmatrix}}_{X} + \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} 0\\ \tfrac{1}{\tau}G\end{pmatrix}}_{B}u(t-T),\qquad y = \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} I & 0\end{pmatrix}}_{C}X,\qquad \Lambda = \operatorname{diag}(a) \]
\[ \frac{y_i(s)}{u_j(s)} = H_{ij}\,g_j\,\frac{e^{-Ts}}{\tau s + 1}\,\frac{a_i}{s + a_i} \]

Steps 5 and 7 stacked. \(u\) enters only the pressure rows.

4 · Sliding mode control

1 · Model (one axis)

\[ \dot y = -a\,y + b\,u + d,\qquad |d| \le D \]

\(b\): input gain. \(d\): unmodelled part, unknown; only its bound \(D\) is assumed. SMC: Utkin 1977; Slotine & Li 1991, ch. 7; Edwards & Spurgeon 1998.

2 · Sliding variable

\[ e = y - r,\qquad \sigma = e\ \ \text{(first-order model)},\qquad \sigma = \dot e + \lambda e\ \ \text{(second-order model)} \]

\(\sigma = 0\) means on target. Second-order case: \(\sigma = 0 \Rightarrow \dot e = -\lambda e \Rightarrow e \propto e^{-\lambda t}\).

3 · Required behaviour of \(\sigma\)

\[ \dot\sigma = -\lambda\,\sigma - \eta\,\operatorname{sign}(\sigma) \]

\(\lambda\): proportional pull to 0. \(\eta\): fixed-size push toward 0, sized to beat \(d\).

4 · Solve for \(u\)

\[ \dot\sigma = -a y + b u + d - \dot r \;\Rightarrow\; u = \frac{1}{b}\Big[\underbrace{\dot r + a y}_{\text{equivalent}} - \underbrace{\lambda\sigma}_{\text{proportional}} - \underbrace{\eta\operatorname{sign}(\sigma)}_{\text{switching}}\Big] \;\Rightarrow\; \dot\sigma = -\lambda\sigma - \eta\operatorname{sign}(\sigma) + d \]

Equivalent: what a perfect model needs. Switching: the term that makes it sliding mode.

5 · Stability (Lyapunov)

\[ V = \tfrac12\sigma^2,\qquad \dot V = \sigma\dot\sigma \le -\lambda\sigma^2 - (\eta - D)\,|\sigma| < 0 \ \ \text{if } \eta > D \]

\(V\) decreases whenever \(\sigma \ne 0\), so \(\sigma \to 0\) for any \(d\) with \(|d| \le D\).

6 · Boundary layer

\[ \operatorname{sign}(\sigma) \;\to\; \operatorname{sat}(\sigma/\mu) = \begin{cases}\sigma/\mu & |\sigma| \le \mu\\ \operatorname{sign}(\sigma) & |\sigma| > \mu\end{cases} \]

Removes chattering of the regulator. Inside \(|\sigma| \le \mu\) the law is linear.

5 · Paper SMC and NDOSMC (on M1)

Model M1

\[ c_i\,\dot y_i + k_i\,y_i = v_i,\quad v = G_1 u \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \dot y_i = -a_i y_i + b_i v_i,\qquad a_i = \tfrac{k_i}{c_i},\ \ b_i = \tfrac{1}{c_i} \]
\[ c = (1.335,\ 1.338,\ 1.165)\ \text{s},\quad k = (0.930,\ 0.924,\ 0.942),\quad G_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 0.000 & -1.983 & -0.002\\ 2.026 & 0.001 & -2.106\\ -0.551 & -0.484 & -0.570\end{pmatrix}\ \tfrac{\text{mm}}{\text{psi}} \]

The paper's model (eq. 2) in tip coordinates: command \(u\) straight to tip \(y\), no dead time, no lag (inner loop assumed fast). \(G_1\): measured static gain from command to tip. \(c, k\): identified at 5 set points; constant, because the pressure-dependence the paper schedules (eq. 17) was not present in \(y_1, y_2\). Data-only tests (scripts/stiffness_vs_pressure.py, M2 mechanics, mean 2–7.5 psi and bend offset ±2 psi): gain ±1 %, rate ±5 % (fit noise); \(y_3\) gain −7 % from 2 to 7.5 psi (not scored).

Paper SMC (eq. 5)

\[ e_i = y_i - r_i,\qquad v_i = \frac{1}{b_i}\Big[\dot r_i + a_i y_i - \lambda e_i - \eta_i\operatorname{sign}(e_i)\Big],\qquad \eta_i = \frac{\delta k_i|y_i| + \delta c_i|\dot y_i|}{c_i} + \epsilon,\qquad u = G_1^{-1}v \]

§4 with \(\sigma = e\). \(\delta k, \delta c\): spread of \(k, c\) across the set points (the paper's bounds). Gains: \(\lambda = 1\) s⁻¹, \(\epsilon = 0.1\) mm/s.

Disturbance observer (eqs. 6–9)

\[ \hat d_i = z_i + l_1 y_i + l_2 y_i|y_i|,\qquad \dot z_i = g_i\big(a_i y_i - b_i v_i - \hat d_i\big),\qquad g_i = l_1 + 2l_2|y_i| \]

\(\hat d\): estimate of the disturbance \(d\) in \(\dot y = -ay + bv + d\). \(z\): observer state. \(l_1, l_2\): observer gains. Then \(\dot{\hat d} = g(d - \hat d)\): the estimate converges at rate \(g\). Sign of the \(\hat d\) term corrected from the printed eq. 7 (it follows from eq. 6).

NDOSMC (eqs. 12, 14)

\[ v_i = \frac{1}{b_i}\Big[\dot r_i + a_i y_i - \lambda e_i - \eta_i\operatorname{sat}(e_i/\mu) - \hat d_i\Big],\qquad \eta_i = \Phi\,e^{-g_i t} + \frac{\epsilon}{g_i},\qquad u = G_1^{-1}v \]

Paper SMC plus \(-\hat d\) and a boundary layer. \(\eta\) only has to cover the observer error. Gains: \(\lambda = 1\), \(l_1 = 0.5\), \(l_2 = 0\), \(\mu = 0.1\) mm, \(\Phi = 1\), \(\epsilon = 1\). Paper's printed \(B = -1/c\) used as \(+1/c\) (needed by its eq. 13).

6 · Delay-aware SMC (on M2)

Paper SMC on M2, plus a Smith predictor for \(T\) and a lag inverse for \(\tau\). Every \(\Delta t\) = 10 ms, step \(k\); \(N = T/\Delta t = 50\).

Once, at the start

\[ \xi_0 = \text{tip after 5 s at 4.5 psi} \;\Rightarrow\; y = \xi - \xi_0;\qquad \hat p_0 = 0,\ \ \hat y_0 = 0 \]

1 · Model without the delay

\[ \hat p_{k+1} = \hat p_k + \tfrac{\Delta t}{\tau}\big(G u_k - \hat p_k\big),\qquad \hat y_{k+1} = \hat y_k + \Delta t\big(-\Lambda\hat y_k + \Lambda H\hat p_k\big) \]

M2 (§2 step 8) driven by \(u_k\) instead of \(u_{k-N}\), Euler steps. \(\hat y_k\) is the model's forecast of \(y_{k+N}\). Store the last \(N\) values of \(\hat y\).

2 · Smith predictor

\[ \tilde y_k = \hat y_k + \big(y_k - \hat y_{k-N}\big) \;\approx\; y_{k+N} \]

\(\hat y_{k-N}\): the model's estimate of \(y_k\), so the bracket is its measured error now. Exact if the model error stays constant over \(T\) (Smith 1957; Normey-Rico & Camacho 2007, ch. 5).

3 · Reference preview

\[ \tilde r_k = r_k + T\dot r_k + \tfrac12 T^2\ddot r_k,\qquad \dot{\tilde r}_k = \dot r_k + T\ddot r_k \]

\(\dot r, \ddot r\): backward differences of the last three \(r\). Target \(T\) ahead, to match \(\tilde y\).

4 · Sliding variable

\[ \sigma_k = \tilde y_k - \tilde r_k \]

5 · SMC law → desired pressure

\[ \dot{\tilde y} = -\Lambda\tilde y + \Lambda H p + d,\quad \dot\sigma = -\lambda\sigma - \eta\operatorname{sat}(\sigma/\mu) \;\Rightarrow\; p_{\text{des}} = H^{-1}\Lambda^{-1}\Big[\dot{\tilde r} + \Lambda\tilde y - \lambda\sigma - \eta\operatorname{sat}(\sigma/\mu)\Big] \]

§4 steps 3–4 with \(b \to \Lambda H\) and input \(p\). Gains: \(\lambda = 8\) s⁻¹, \(\eta = 1\) mm/s, \(\mu = 1\) mm.

6 · Lag inverse → command

\[ \tau\dot p = -p + Gu \;\Rightarrow\; u_k = G^{-1}\big(p_{\text{des}} + \tau\,\dot p_{\text{des}}\big),\qquad P^{\text{cmd}} = \operatorname{clip}(4.5 + u_k,\ 0,\ 9) \]

\(\dot p_{\text{des},k} = (p_{\text{des},k} - p_{\text{des},k-1})/\Delta t\): unfiltered backward difference of step 5's output (0 at \(k = 0\)). Gives \(p(t) = p_{\text{des}}(t - T)\) when unclipped; step 2 accounts for the \(T\). Send \(P^{\text{cmd}}\) (S2, S3, S4), return to step 1 with \(u_k\).

Optional: observer

\[ p_{\text{des}} \;\to\; p_{\text{des}} - H^{-1}\Lambda^{-1}\hat d,\qquad \hat d \text{ from §5 with } y \to \tilde y,\ \ b\,v \to \Lambda H p \]

Delay-aware NDOSMC. Gains: \(\lambda = 4\), \(l_1 = 0.1\), \(\mu = 1\) mm, \(\Phi = 1\), \(\epsilon = 1\).

Code: DelayAwareNDOSMC in scripts/ndosmc.py (observer=False for the delay-aware SMC).
