Soft pneumatic arm · open-loop plant · make_sim()

What goes in, what is remembered, what comes out

The plant is everything between the 20 pressure commands and the measured tip position, with no controller inside it. Values come from docs/simulator.md and scripts/plant_model.py, which were read from the simulator source and checked against the running sim.

Is u 20 long?

u ∈ ℝ²⁰  (yes, in the simulator)
  • One absolute pressure command per pouch, 0–9 psi. 4 columns (S1–S4) × 5 levels (P1–P5), flattened S1P1 … S4P5.
  • Only 15 of the 20 directions move the arm. At each level the 4 pressures make 3 things: a bend about x, a bend about y and a stretch. Raising S1 and S3 while lowering S2 and S4 by the same amount does nothing.
  • The real lab arm may have only 3 driven inputs plus a sealed sensing column (open question in hardware.md).

What is the state?

x = [q, q̇, p] ∈ ℝ⁵⁰
  • q (15): joint coordinates of the 5-link chain. Each level has a stretch ext k and two bends bx k, by k.
  • q̇ (15): their rates. The mechanics are second order, so both are needed.
  • p (20): the actual pouch pressures after the 0.6 s lag. They lag behind u, so they carry memory.
  • For tip x, y only about 4–5 states per axis matter (Hankel singular values 1, 0.235, 0.091, 0.036, 0.0036).

Per level: x-bend ∝ S1 − S3, y-bend ∝ S2 − S4, stretch ∝ the sum of all four. That's 3 effective inputs per level, 15 in all. Pouches near the mount move the tip more: 8 psi on S1 P1 moved it 3.4 mm, against 1.4 mm for S1 P5.
